# Pricing workshop: moving to three tiers with unlimited viewers

## Summary
The group agreed to move from per-seat pricing (39 EUR per seat per month) to three tiers priced by editors and connected data sources, with unlimited viewers. The new pricing would be announced in January together with the exports launch. Mira builds the revenue model by Thursday; Sam tests the framing in three customer calls.

## Pricing model
- Per-seat pricing discourages customers from inviting more people; viewers consume value, editors create it.
- Levers: number of editors and number of connected data sources (customers understand sources; accounts with two or more sources rarely churn).
- Rough tier sketch from Mira: Starter at about 290 EUR with two sources, Growth at about 900 EUR with up to ten sources and scheduled exports, Enterprise on custom quote.
- Enterprise includes SSO, dedicated support and the Snowflake connector, with a minimum annual commitment.

## Transition and trial
- Growth would be a price increase for about a quarter of customers; grandfather existing customers for 12 months.
- Free trial grows from 14 to 30 days, limited to two data sources.
- Annual billing gets two months free.

## Decisions
- Three tiers, unlimited viewers, editors and data sources as the two pricing levers.
- 30-day trial limited to two data sources; annual discount of two months.
- Announce in January with the exports launch; 12 months grandfathering.
